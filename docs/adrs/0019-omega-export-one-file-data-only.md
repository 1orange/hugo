# ADR 0019 — The Omega export is one data-only file per company-month

Date: 2026-09-27
Status: Accepted
Supersedes: ADR 0014's choice of `T00` for every document type, its accounts position, and its
partner handling
Amends: ADR 0013 (the cash-versus-card distinction no longer decides anything)
Relates to: ADR 0010 (only confirmed documents export), ADR 0017, ADR 0018, PRD 0002

## Context

ADR 0014 settled on the `T00` (EUD) text format for everything and left its hardest questions to
"one export from her own Omega". There is now one: `docs/reference/omega/eport OF .txt`, produced by
following KROS's own FAQ, for her firm `spring` in May 2026. It is a **`T01` (Fakturácia)** export
of eleven issued invoices, and six of them are the PDFs in `spring/2026_05/01 Vystavené faktúry`.
Joined on invoice number, all six agree with their PDF on VS, issue date, DUZP, due date, customer
IČO, base, VAT and total.

Reading the file against the vendored specification (`ImportExport_28_00_2025.xls`) with its cell
colouring, alongside KROS's help pages:

- **Items from the `>>` marker onward are optional** — stated in the spec's general sheet — and the
  marker sits in different places in the two formats.
  - **`T01`**: header fields up to the document type are mandatory — number, partner name, IČO, the
    issue, due and DUZP dates, bases and VAT per rate slot, rates, rounding and total. **The document
    type itself is optional and defaults to 0, an issued invoice**; a received invoice is 14. Item
    fields up to the unit price are mandatory. **Accounts are optional.**
  - **`T00`**: the header's evidence code and number series (which *"musí byť definovaná v
    Omege"*), internal number, external number, partner, all five dates, currency, rates and sums
    are mandatory. The accounting-entry item (`typ položky` 0) carries **MD and DAL accounts before
    `>>`, so they are mandatory.**
- **`T01` has no cash documents.** Its types are invoices, orders, delivery notes and the like, so
  receipts can only go through `T00`.
- **`T00` does not create partners:** *"Ak nie je definovaný v číselníku partnerov, Omega ho
  automaticky nepridá."*
- **One file may hold several data types**, each section starting with its own `R00` line.
- **Decimal separators are mixed within a row** in the export — dots in header amounts and item
  prices, commas in the item prices *including* VAT and the KV DPH quantity. Every comma-formatted
  field is optional and derived.
- **Re-import is a choice in the import dialogue**: *Pridať neexistujúce doklady* (the default),
  *Prepísať existujúce*, or both.
- KROS's own product for this workflow, Digitálna kancelária, imports documents into Omega
  **unposted by default** and she posts them there. Unposted import is normal practice.

Omega's VAT-deduction register for the same month shows how she actually books:

- Her evidence codes are `DF` for received invoices, `FDD` for tax documents to an advance payment,
  **`IDk` for card receipts booked as internal documents**, and `zDF` for foreign ones.
- **The folder does not decide the document's type.** The Bolt taxi invoice sits in
  `05 Bločky_firemná karta` and is booked as `DF` 3026032.

And one scope finding: `spring`'s issued invoices are **generated by Omega itself** (*"Vytvorené v
programe OMEGA"*). They are already in her books; importing them again would duplicate them.

Finally, the app's scope was restated: **it parses documents and hands the data to Omega. Nothing
else.** Choosing accounts, posting, evidence codes, KV DPH sections and cost centres is her work,
done in Omega. An expense-account picker was proposed and rejected on exactly that ground.

## Decision

**One TXT file per company per month**, three sections in this order:

1. **`R00 T04` — partners.** Every counterparty the documents below refer to, with name, address,
   IČO, DIČ and IČ DPH, filled from the registers by IČO (ADR 0018), from the eKasa lookup's
   `organization` for receipts (ADR 0016), and from the document alone for foreign partners. Placing
   them first means a `T00` document never names a partner Omega does not know.
2. **`R00 T01` — invoices.** Issued invoices as type 0, received invoices as type 14.
3. **`R00 T00` — receipts**, with one default document type from settings.

**The section is chosen by the document's type, not its folder**: an eKasa receipt, or a document
whose `docTypeHint` is `receipt`, goes to `T00`; an invoice goes to `T01` wherever it was filed. She
can override it on the document.

**The file carries data, never accounting.** No accounts, no `typ DPH`, no KV DPH section, no cost
centre, order or activity codes, no posting templates. Where the format demands a code, the app
writes a default from settings and she changes it in Omega. **The cash-versus-card distinction
leaves the app entirely**: ADR 0013 kept it only because it "decides the target ledger on import",
and she now decides that in Omega.

Format rules:

- Windows-1250, TAB separators, CRLF line endings, dates as `DD.MM.RRRR`.
- **Every amount is written with a dot.** Fields the export shows with a comma are optional and
  derived, so the writer leaves them empty rather than reproduce a mixed convention.
- Mandatory fields are always written; optional fields only when the payload has a value.
- VAT goes into Omega's **fixed rate slots** — lower (19), higher (23), reduced 2 (5), zero and
  exempt — with **one item per VAT rate** rather than per line item (ADR 0017).
- String lengths must fit Omega's column sizes, per the spec's warning sheet. **Free text** —
  partner names, item text — is shortened to fit; real names do overflow, such as the 95-character
  legal name of ŠEVT a.s. against a 75-character column. **Identifiers, numbers and amounts are
  never shortened**: a document whose identifier would not fit is held back with the reason stated,
  rather than written wrong. This refines PRD 0001's "fails rather than truncates", which was
  written before a real overflowing name was seen.

Numbering:

- **Every document gets a stable app-assigned number with a distinctive prefix** (for example
  `H2605-0001`: the company's month and a sequence) that never changes once assigned. The mandatory
  number field is never left for Omega to fill, because the spec gives no sign that it will.
- The **prefix is a safety property**: under *Pridať neexistujúce*, an app number colliding with a
  document she typed by hand would silently skip the app's document.
- The **supplier's own number** travels separately: in `T00` as the external number and the KV DPH
  document number, in `T01` as `VS`.
- Evidence and number-series codes default to her usage — `DF`, `IDk` and so on — as one global
  setting.

What goes in:

- **Every confirmed document of the month**, and nothing else (ADR 0010). Regenerating a month's file
  is therefore safe: stable numbers plus *Pridať neexistujúce* make a second import skip what the
  first one brought in, and late arrivals simply appear the next time.
- Her own firm's Omega-generated invoices are marked **not relevant — already in Omega** and never
  reach the file. No per-company flag: she rejected one, and the export gate already covers it.
- Foreign-currency documents use Omega's foreign-currency types (`zDF`, `zPD`, `zID`) and carry
  currency and amount. **The app never invents an exchange rate.**

## Consequences

- **The export can be built and tested without the model.** It works from confirmed payloads, so
  eKasa receipts and anything she typed reach Omega before invoice extraction exists.
- **The `T01` writer has a golden file.** The six matched `spring` invoices can be generated and
  diffed against her export's data columns, verifying column positions and formats without an
  import.
- **Receipts may still be blocked by the format.** `T00` marks MD and DAL mandatory; whether an
  empty value imports is unknown. If it does not, the options are a single constant account pair she
  nominates as a format filler, or leaving receipts out of the file. Neither involves the app
  choosing accounts.
- **The test import is deferred by her choice.** The writer is built to the spec and the sample, and
  the unknowns below stay open until the first real import.
- ADR 0014's consequences about non-transactional import, the `.LOG` file being the only honest
  confirmation, and string lengths still hold.
- The deletion of cash versus card removes a derived field and its tests from ADR 0013's model.
  Nothing stored changes, because it was never stored.

### Unknowns, to be settled by the first real import

1. Whether a `T01` received invoice (type 14) imports with no accounts and no series codes, and
   where it lands.
2. Whether a prefixed number that does not follow the series counter is accepted.
3. Whether a `T00` receipt imports with MD and DAL empty.
4. Whether a `T00` receipt naming a new partner imports once a `T04` section precedes it.
5. Whether importing the same file twice skips everything the second time.
6. Whether dot decimals produce correct amounts in every field written.
7. Whether a foreign-currency document imports with the rate fields empty, letting Omega fill them
   from its own exchange-rate list.

Plus, carried from ADR 0014: whether Omega 29.20 accepts a file built to the 28.00 spec. The `T01`
columns in her export line up with the 28.00 spec by name and position, which is evidence for `T01`
and none for `T00`. And, new: how Omega treats a **Czech company** whose books are in CZK, given the
spec's rate slots are Slovak.

## Alternatives considered

- **`T00` for everything**, as ADR 0014 decided. One format, but it requires accounts on every
  invoice, which puts accounting judgement into the app.
- **Two files per company-month**, invoices and receipts separately. Proposed first; no safer,
  because the import is not transactional and the `.LOG` reports per line. One file is one import.
- **An expense-account picker with per-supplier memory.** Rejected: accounting stays in Omega.
- **A per-company "issues invoices in Omega" flag** making `01` presence-only. Rejected by her; her
  own firm is the only such company and the export gate already handles it.
- **Leaving the number for Omega to assign.** The spec marks it mandatory and nothing says Omega
  fills it.
- **Importing as payments (`T08` Úhrady).** Export-only (ADR 0014).
